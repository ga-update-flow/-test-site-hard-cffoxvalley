Helen Bader Institute for Nonprofit Management

Mission and Vision

The Helen Bader Institute for Nonprofit Management at the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee strives to improve the leadership and effectiveness of nonprofit organizations through education, research, and service.

Our vision is to be recognized as one of the top ten nonprofit academic centers in the United States, enhancing the nonprofit sector and the quality of life for all.
